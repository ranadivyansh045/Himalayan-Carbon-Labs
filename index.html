The problem

A $2B market that
Himalayan communities can't reach

India is a top-5 source of carbon credits, yet MRV remains manual, slow and expensive. Project development costs of $50,000–$200,000 keep small farmers and Van Panchayats locked out of the very market their forests sustain.

71%

Forest cover across 45,000+ km² — a massive, unmonetised carbon sink.

12,000+

Van Panchayats managing ~14% of forest area with zero carbon market access.

3,000+

Forest fire incidents every year — pine needles fuel them, no early warning exists.

10%

Of villages nearly empty due to outmigration. Carbon revenue can reverse it.

Himalaya Carbon OS

Four layers. One verifiable truth.

From satellite pixels to settled payments — an end-to-end stack built for integrity, auditability and scale.

L1

Data Collection

Satellite imagery, drone LiDAR, IoT soil sensors, farmer mobile app and ground-truth field plots — fused into one continuous data stream.

Sentinel · Planet · SAR · LoRaWAN
L2

Data Processing

Cloud pipelines with PostGIS and COG storage, ML models for biomass, soil carbon and canopy height, plus full uncertainty quantification.

AWS · GCP · CNN · Transformer
L3

MRV Engine

Carbon stock calculation aligned to IPCC and Verra methodologies, additionality and leakage analysis, and blockchain-backed audit trails.

VM0042 · VM0015 · VM0047
L4

Marketplace

Credit issuance, buyer matching, smart-contract settlement via UPI and bank rails, and village-level traceability dashboards for buyers.
